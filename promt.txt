# Project: Automated Hindu Festival Research & AI Documentary Video Generator

Act as a Senior Python Engineer, AI Video Generation Architect, Research Engineer, and DevOps Specialist.

I want you to build a complete, production-ready, fully automated AI video generation codebase.

Reference repository:
https://github.com/rishuwrites/serial-recap-bot

Study the reference repository's architecture, automation approach, modular code organization, and workflow wherever accessible. Use it as inspiration, but build an entirely new system specifically for Hindu festival research and documentary video production.

Do not just provide a plan or sample code. Build the complete working repository with all necessary source files, configurations, GitHub Actions workflows, tests, documentation, and error handling.

## 1. Main Objective

The system should accept just one input:

Festival Name: Diwali / Holi / Chhath Puja / Navratri / Ram Navami / Janmashtami / Makar Sankranti / Maha Shivratri / Durga Puja / etc.

After receiving the festival name, the system must automatically:

1. Research the festival in depth.
2. Collect information from multiple independent and trustworthy sources.
3. Cross-check facts and resolve conflicting information.
4. Generate a detailed documentary script using Google Gemini.
5. Divide the script into timed scenes.
6. Generate relevant images using multiple AI image-generation providers.
7. Generate Hindi narration using Microsoft Edge TTS.
8. Add animated overlays, transitions, and motion effects.
9. Synchronize images, narration, subtitles, and animations.
10. Render a complete documentary video of at least 6 minutes.
11. Export the final MP4 video and all supporting files as GitHub Actions artifacts.

The entire pipeline must run automatically through GitHub Actions.

## 2. Deep Research Engine

Research quality is extremely important.

Do not depend on a single website or source.

Use multiple research sources, including:

- Google Search results.
- Wikipedia (background reference only).
- Government and tourism websites.
- Ministry of Culture, Government of India.
- Official temple and religious institution websites.
- Authentic historical references.
- Academic articles and research publications.
- Sanskrit and Hindu scripture references where relevant.
- Reputable newspapers and cultural publications.

Research must cover:

1. Origin and history of the festival.
2. Historical background and evolution.
3. Religious and spiritual significance.
4. References in Hindu scriptures, Puranas, and traditional texts.
5. Associated gods and goddesses.
6. Mythological stories and legends.
7. Regional celebrations across India.
8. Traditional rituals and their meanings.
9. Puja procedures and important customs.
10. Traditional foods and offerings.
11. Cultural and social significance.
12. Scientific or seasonal explanations, where supported by evidence.
13. Important dates and astronomical calculations, where relevant.
14. How the festival is celebrated in modern India.

### Research reliability requirements

- Use at least 5 independent sources where available.
- Prefer primary and authoritative sources.
- Store source URLs and publication information.
- Cross-check important historical and religious claims.
- Never invent scripture references, quotations, dates, or historical facts.
- Clearly distinguish mythology, religious tradition, historical evidence, and modern interpretation.
- If information is disputed, explain the disagreement instead of presenting one interpretation as established fact.

Save research output as structured JSON and Markdown.

## 3. Gemini Script Generation Engine

Use Google Gemini API as the primary intelligence and script-generation engine.

The system should generate an engaging Hindi documentary script.

Requirements:

- Minimum video duration: 6 minutes.
- Target duration: 7–9 minutes.
- Language: Natural, fluent Hindi.
- Tone: Professional Indian documentary narration.
- Storytelling: Cinematic, emotional, informative, and engaging.
- Avoid repetitive explanations.
- Include a powerful opening hook.
- Build curiosity throughout the documentary.
- Include meaningful transitions between sections.
- End with a memorable conclusion.

The script must be based on collected research, not general AI knowledge alone.

Generate:

- Documentary title.
- Video description.
- Complete Hindi narration.
- Scene-by-scene breakdown.
- Narration timestamps.
- Image-generation prompts.
- Animation instructions.
- Subtitle text.
- Source references.

Use a second Gemini model or configurable fallback provider when the primary model is unavailable.

## 4. AI Image Generation Engine – Multiple Providers

This is a critical requirement.

Generate approximately ONE relevant image for every 10 seconds of video.

For a 6-minute documentary, the target is at least 36 image assets.

For an 8-minute documentary, the target is approximately 48 image assets.

Each image must visually correspond to the narration of its scene.

Support multiple image-generation providers through independent adapters.

Investigate and integrate providers with usable free tiers or free local inference options, where available.

Potential integrations:

- Pollinations AI, subject to current availability and terms.
- Hugging Face Inference providers.
- Cloud-based open-model image generation APIs.
- Configurable Stable Diffusion-compatible endpoints.
- Local Stable Diffusion or other open-source image models when GPU resources are available.

Important:

Do not assume that every provider is permanently free. Check provider availability, authentication requirements, rate limits, and licensing.

### Automatic image fallback

Implement provider priority and automatic fallback.

Example:

Provider A → Provider B → Provider C → Local model → Retry queue.

If one provider fails, automatically try the next provider.

Additional requirements:

- Retry with exponential backoff.
- Maintain image-generation logs.
- Save prompts and provider information.
- Validate downloaded image files.
- Avoid duplicate images.
- Generate images in 16:9 format.
- Maintain consistent visual style across scenes.
- Prefer historically and culturally accurate Indian visual representations.
- Avoid incorrect religious symbols, deities, clothing, and rituals.

If image generation fails, use a configurable fallback image-generation or stock-image strategy rather than silently creating broken scenes.

## 5. Hindi Voice Generation – Microsoft Edge TTS

Use Microsoft Edge TTS as the default narration engine.

Requirements:

- Natural Hindi narration.
- Prefer suitable Indian Hindi voices.
- Configurable male and female voices.
- Adjustable speech rate and pitch.
- Generate audio scene by scene.
- Save individual scene audio files.
- Merge all audio into one narration track.
- Automatically calculate audio duration.
- Avoid unnecessary silence.
- Synchronize narration with video scenes.

The system must support retrying failed TTS requests.

Keep TTS independent from the image and research modules.
